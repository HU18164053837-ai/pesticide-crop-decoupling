# Data redistribution audit

Checked16November2026 against official sources:

- [FAO database terms](https://www.fao.org/contact-us/terms/db-terms-of-use/en):permit reuse and redistribution under CC BY4.0 plus additional terms,unless dataset metadata states otherwise. The frozen RP/QV/RL/QI extracts and derived panels retain attribution,original flags and source hashes. No logo/endorsement claim is used.
- [World Bank terms](https://data.worldbank.org/summary-terms-of-use):allow copying/adaptation under CC BY4.0 plus attribution and mandatory additional terms,subject to indicator/provider exceptions. Archived metadata for NY.GDP.PCAP.KD,NV.AGR.TOTL.KD and AG.PRD.CROP.XD identifies the providers;the latter traces to FAO. No extra redistribution restriction was identified in those metadata. Provider obligations remain applicable.

Included:country-level public observations,selected source rows,processed analytic panels,nonpersonal classification/quality metadata and study outputs. Excluded:full raw bulk ZIP databases,copyrighted article PDFs,manuscript histories,credentials and private information. The general source terms are linked rather than copied as extensive copyrighted text. No independent blanket licence is assigned to third-party data.

Official source URLs,updates,retrieval date6November2026,raw hashes and units are in data/metadata/source_register.csv. The underlying archived bulk files can be matched by hash if separately available;no new database version was substituted. These checks support the stated sharing basis and retain rather than waive any third-party rights.
